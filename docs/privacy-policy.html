TANGHAP Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

TANGHAP (Intelligent Inhaler Monitoring) is an Android app and inhaler attachment developed by the TANGHAP Project Team for academic research. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can have it deleted. It follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

TANGHAP provides respiratory monitoring and a preliminary, AI-assisted assessment only. It does not diagnose any disease and does not replace a doctor.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name, email address, username, age and birthday.
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive only your name and email address from that service. We never receive your Google or Facebook password, and we do not post anything to your accounts.

Health profile (entered by you)

  • Biological sex, height and weight.
  • Smoking or vaping, and exposure to air pollution.
  • Diagnosed respiratory conditions and diagnosis date, previous hospitalization, and family history of respiratory illness.
  • Cough frequency and type, and shortness of breath.
  • Inhaler type, inhaler use per day, prescribed doses per day, medications and allergies.

Emergency contact (entered by you)

  • The name, relationship and phone number of the person you choose.

Monitoring sessions (from the TANGHAP inhaler attachment)

  • Inhalation pressure readings, breathing rate, inhalation duration and inhaler presses.
  • Cough count and the cough type detected (dry, wet, persistent or severe cough, or wheezing), with the model's confidence.
  • The preliminary assessment, observations and over-the-counter guidance shown to you.

Sound from the inhaler attachment

  • The attachment's microphone sends sound to your phone over Bluetooth during a session.
  • The sound is analyzed on your phone by the built-in cough model. Audio recordings are not saved and are never uploaded. Only the result (for example "Wet cough, 87% confidence") is kept.

App and device information

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and basic app usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), which include device model, Android version and an app instance identifier.
  • The Bluetooth address of your TANGHAP attachment, stored only on your phone so it can reconnect.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location. Android asks for location permission only because older Android versions require it to scan for Bluetooth devices. The "Nearby hospitals" button opens your map app, which handles the search itself.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos or messages.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To measure your inhaler sessions and show results, history, trends and alerts.
  • To personalize the preliminary assessment using your health profile.
  • To remind you about doses and let you call your emergency contact.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where your information is stored

  • On your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • In Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), so your account and session history are kept when you change phones. Firebase is operated by Google and may store data outside the Philippines.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase: stores your account and data, and provides crash reports and usage statistics on our behalf.
  • Google or Meta (Facebook): only to sign you in, if you choose that sign-in method.
  • People you choose: the doctor report (PDF) is shared only when you send it yourself, and the emergency button only opens your phone's dialer.

We may disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and all of its data as described below.

6. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time. See how to delete your data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on that phone.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, and object to its processing. You can edit most of your information in the app under Settings → Edit Profile. For anything else, email us.

8. Security

Data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit, and your data is stored under your own account. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Users under 18 should use TANGHAP with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
