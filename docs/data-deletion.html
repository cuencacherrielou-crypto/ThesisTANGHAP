Delete your TANGHAP data

TANGHAP (Intelligent Inhaler Monitoring) by the TANGHAP Project Team

Request deletion of your account and data

  1. Send an email to Tanghap@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my TANGHAP account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to TANGHAP, or include that email address and the sign-in method you use (email and password, Google, or Facebook).
  3. We will delete your account and all of its data and reply to confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address, username, age and birthday).
  • Your health profile and emergency contact.
  • All of your inhaler monitoring sessions and results.

Anonymous crash reports kept by Firebase Crashlytics cannot be linked back to you and are removed automatically by Firebase.

Remove data from your phone

Uninstall TANGHAP. This removes everything the app stored on that phone.

If you signed in with Facebook

You can also remove TANGHAP's access in Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, select TANGHAP and choose Remove. This stops TANGHAP from receiving your Facebook information. To delete the data already stored by TANGHAP, also send the email above.
